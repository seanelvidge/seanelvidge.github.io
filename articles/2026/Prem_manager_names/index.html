Frankly, Graham, That's Quite a Coincidence

November 21, 2026 • Sean Elvidge

2026   ·   football  

Today I watched Coventry City, managed by Frank Lampard, beat Crystal Palace, managed by Thomas Frank (3-2, shameless plug for my football database). At the start of the match my friend sitting next to me asked:

Has a Premier League manager ever faced another manager whose surname was the same as their own first name before today?

We knew this wasn’t the first time these two had faced off before and they had met several times before (including in April 2023 when Lampard’s Chelsea lost 2–0 to Frank’s Brentford).

But surely there must be others?

Graham versus Graham

It turns out there is at least one more example, and for this we need to go back to the 1999/2000 season.

Tottenham Hotspur were managed by George Graham, while newly promoted Watford were managed by Graham Taylor.

Which meant, on Boxing Day 1999, we were treated to Graham versus Graham.

(Obviously) the two managers met twice that season:

Date Match Result
26 December 1999 Tottenham vs Watford 4–0
25 March 2000 Watford vs Tottenham 1–1

So Graham finished the season unbeaten against Graham, winning one and drawing the other.

Or, depending on which Graham you’re talking about, he failed to win either match.

The Near Misses

George Graham also faced Graeme Souness, but unfortunately Graeme and Graham are spelt differently. Steve Bruce and Bruce Rioch both managed Premier League clubs, as did Alex Neil and Neil Warnock. Unfortunately, their respective spells didn’t overlap, so neither pairing actually met.

That leaves us with just two pairings: Frank Lampard versus Thomas Frank, and George Graham versus Graham Taylor.
