I am [Professor of Space Environment at the University of Birmingham](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/staff/profiles/eese/elvidge-sean) and lead the [Space Environment and Radio Engineering (SERENE)](https://serene.bham.ac.uk/) research group. My work uses mathematical modelling to understand the space environment and improve forecasts of its effects on technology.

This is the accessible side of that work: explanations of solar storms and their consequences, the statistics of rare events, modelling resources, and occasional experiments in communicating the data differently. For academic work, see my [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}), [University research profile](https://research.birmingham.ac.uk/en/persons/sean-elvidge/) and [ORCID record](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-2846-0730).
