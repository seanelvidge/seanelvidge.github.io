Much of this website begins with a deceptively simple question and ends with a probability distribution, a model, or an unexpectedly long calculation. This collection brings together recreational mathematics, statistics, Bayesian modelling and puzzles, with routes into their applications in [football]({{ '/football/' | relative_url }}) and [space weather]({{ '/space-weather/' | relative_url }}).

## Bayesian models and forecasting

My [Kalman filter resources and tutorial notes]({{ '/kalman/' | relative_url }}) connect Bayes' theorem to estimating a changing system from noisy observations. Try the bouncing-ball example, then see the same family of ideas used in [football team-strength modelling]({% post_url 2025-12-15-Football_team_rankings %}).

For a model-validation example, read [how I compared Euro 2020 predictions]({% post_url 2021-06-15-Euro_2020_Round_1_Results_and_Validation %}) alongside [the final results]({% post_url 2021-07-14-Euro_2020_Final_Modelling_Results %}).
